# Metalenz

> Metalenz is a fabless semiconductor optics company in Boston, Massachusetts that commercialises
> meta-optics — flat metasurface lenses that replace stacks of curved refractive elements with a
> single planar chip, patterned by nanostructures and mass produced in a standard semiconductor
> foundry. Founded in 2016 by Rob Devlin and Federico Capasso out of the Capasso Lab at Harvard,
> holding the exclusive worldwide licence to that lab's metasurface IP.

## What Metalenz sells

Optical components, IP licences and full-stack biometric solutions — to device makers and
semiconductor foundries, not to developers.

- **PolarEyes** — the polarization imaging platform the products sit on
- **Polar ID** — single-camera polarization face authentication for smartphones, working under a
  fully powered-on OLED display; uses the Samsung ISOCELL Vizion 931 sensor and the Qualcomm
  Snapdragon imaging stack
- **Polar 3D** — on-device relightable selfies and avatars from a single image
- **Orion** / **Gemini** — dot-pattern projectors for structured-light 3D sensing

Manufacturing and licensing partners named publicly: STMicroelectronics, UMC.
Investors named publicly: Neotribe Ventures, Intel Capital, TDK Ventures, 3M, Applied Ventures,
M Ventures, Braemar Energy Ventures, Foothill Ventures.

## How to consume it

- Anonymous. No key, no token, no account. `Allow: GET`.
- `per_page` is 1..100; out of bounds returns `400 rest_invalid_param` with the exact bound.
- Page by the RFC 8288 `Link: rel="next"` header. `X-WP-Total` / `X-WP-TotalPages` give counts.
- Send `_fields=` to trim responses; a full page record is ~22KB.
- Add `_embed` to inline author, featured media and terms in one round trip.
- Errors are the WordPress envelope `{code, message, data:{status}}` as `application/json` — **not**
  RFC 9457 problem+json.
- **401 means stop, not authenticate.** There is no public credential path; the only method the
  server declares is WordPress application passwords, issued from the site admin.
- `POST /batch/v1` is blocked at the Fastly/Pantheon edge with a plain-text `403 Request has been
  blocked.` — handle a non-JSON 4xx body.
- No rate-limit headers of any family, no `Retry-After`, no 429 observed. Self-throttle.
- Responses are `cache-control: public, max-age=604800` behind Fastly. Treat as an archive, not a
  feed.
- **`/wp/v2/users` returns real people.** It answers anonymously. It is documented structurally in
  this profile and deliberately excluded from every skill and tool here. Do not enumerate it.
